A view into North Korea through binoculars. If you look very carefully, you can see workers in the paddy fields. At the Dora observatory the following facts are known: 1) These farmers rarely use tractors, probably because gasoline shortages. 2) Ditto fertiliser 3) Due to these organic farming practices, the rice tends to crop late, and yields are low.
