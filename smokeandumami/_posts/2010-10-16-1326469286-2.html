And so it is goodbye, Korea, hello Tok-y-o! A late morning flight from the spectacular-looking Incheon airport was quick and easy. We’ve just arrived at our new home for the next few days, the 37th floor of the Keio Plaza Hotel in Shinjuku. In this case, settling in means setting up Internet Connection Sharing (Access point: LOST_IN_TRANSLATION), having a shower and finding out where to head for eating and photo opportunities. After that’s done, I am sure we will sleep very deeply tonight.
