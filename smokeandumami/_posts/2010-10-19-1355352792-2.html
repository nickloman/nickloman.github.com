Part-way through the journey to Kyoto we decided instead to continue to Osaka and spend the evening there. Osaka has the archetype “BladeRunner” scene-scape, making it a must-see destination. We are getting adept at navigating the subway system. It isn’t usually hard, stations are both named and numbered meaning it is easy to know if you are going in the right direction.
